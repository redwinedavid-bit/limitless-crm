Turn automatic target pricing off to use an exception. Saving records your approval, price and reason in the audit history. Missing costs and minimum call charges cannot be bypassed. Further discounts require a new reviewed exception.

With automatic pricing on, the slider fills any price shortfall through the Service call charge. The adjustment is shown below. Existing parts and hourly prices are retained when they already exceed the target. Review before exporting. Saving a draft does not send a quote or issue an invoice.

Gross margin = (pre-tax selling price − direct cost) ÷ pre-tax selling price. Business overhead and income tax are not deducted here. A 35% markup produces a smaller margin than 35%.
